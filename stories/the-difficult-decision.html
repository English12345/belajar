The Difficult Decision

Keputusan yang Sulit

💡 Cerita ini level lanjutan — kosakatanya lebih kaya. Klik kata apa pun untuk melihat artinya, atau tekan "Lihat Arti Cerita" untuk terjemahan tiap paragraf sekaligus.

For nearly a decade, Farah had built a reputation as one of the most reliable analysts at the firm. Her desk overlooked the city, her salary was generous, and her career trajectory seemed, by every conventional measure, enviable. Yet lately, she had been consumed by a persistent, nagging restlessness that she could neither explain nor ignore.

Selama hampir sepuluh tahun, Farah membangun reputasi sebagai salah satu analis paling andal di perusahaan itu. Mejanya menghadap ke kota, gajinya besar, dan jenjang kariernya tampak, menurut ukuran konvensional apa pun, patut diirikan. Namun belakangan ini, dia terus dihantui kegelisahan yang membandel dan tak kunjung reda, yang tak bisa dia jelaskan maupun abaikan.

It had started, she supposed, with a chance encounter at a conference six months earlier. A former colleague, Rizal, had left the same firm two years prior to launch his own consultancy. He spoke about his venture with an infectious enthusiasm that Farah had not felt about her own work in a long time. "I'm terrified most days," he admitted, laughing, "but I've never felt more alive."

Semuanya bermula, menurutnya, dari pertemuan tak terduga di sebuah konferensi enam bulan sebelumnya. Seorang mantan rekan kerja, Rizal, telah keluar dari perusahaan yang sama dua tahun sebelumnya untuk membangun konsultannya sendiri. Dia bercerita tentang usahanya dengan antusiasme yang menular, sesuatu yang sudah lama tidak dirasakan Farah terhadap pekerjaannya sendiri. "Aku takut hampir setiap hari," akunya sambil tertawa, "tapi aku tidak pernah merasa sehidup ini."

Since that conversation, Farah had quietly begun sketching out a business plan of her own: an independent consultancy focused on sustainable supply chains, an area she had grown passionate about but had never been able to pursue fully within the constraints of her current role. Late at night, after her daughter had gone to bed, she would sit at the kitchen table, spreadsheets glowing on her laptop screen, weighing projected revenue against the terrifying certainty of giving up a stable paycheck.

Sejak percakapan itu, diam-diam Farah mulai menyusun rencana bisnisnya sendiri: sebuah konsultan independen yang berfokus pada rantai pasok berkelanjutan, bidang yang sangat dia minati namun tak pernah bisa dia tekuni sepenuhnya dalam batasan peran pekerjaannya saat ini. Larut malam, setelah putrinya tidur, dia akan duduk di meja dapur, spreadsheet menyala di layar laptopnya, menimbang proyeksi pendapatan melawan kepastian menakutkan dari melepaskan gaji tetap.

Her husband, Adi, was supportive in principle but understandably cautious. "I believe in you completely," he told her one evening, "but we have a mortgage, and Sarah starts secondary school next year. Have you thought through the worst-case scenario?" His question was not meant to discourage her; it was, she knew, a reasonable one. Still, it lingered in her mind for days.

Suaminya, Adi, mendukung secara prinsip tetapi wajar jika berhati-hati. "Aku sepenuhnya percaya padamu," katanya suatu malam, "tapi kita punya cicilan rumah, dan Sarah mulai SMP tahun depan. Sudahkah kamu memikirkan skenario terburuknya?" Pertanyaannya bukan dimaksudkan untuk mematahkan semangatnya; itu, dia tahu, adalah pertanyaan yang masuk akal. Meski begitu, pertanyaan itu terus terngiang di benaknya selama berhari-hari.

Farah spent the following weeks conducting exhaustive research. She interviewed a dozen entrepreneurs, several of whom had failed spectacularly before eventually succeeding. Their stories were sobering, filled with anecdotes of depleted savings and strained relationships, but also, unexpectedly, a shared sense of conviction that they would make the same choice again if given the chance.

Farah menghabiskan minggu-minggu berikutnya melakukan riset yang menyeluruh. Dia mewawancarai belasan pengusaha, beberapa di antaranya pernah gagal total sebelum akhirnya berhasil. Kisah-kisah mereka sungguh mengingatkan, dipenuhi anekdot tentang tabungan yang terkuras dan hubungan yang tegang, tetapi juga, di luar dugaan, ada rasa keyakinan yang sama bahwa mereka akan mengambil pilihan yang sama lagi jika diberi kesempatan.

One particularly candid mentor, a woman named Ratna who had built and later sold a logistics startup, offered her a piece of advice that stuck with Farah long after their conversation ended. "Don't ask yourself whether you'll regret leaving," Ratna said. "Ask yourself whether you'll regret never having tried."

Salah satu mentor yang sangat terbuka, seorang wanita bernama Ratna yang pernah membangun dan kemudian menjual startup logistik, memberinya sebuah nasihat yang terus terngiang di benak Farah lama setelah percakapan mereka berakhir. "Jangan tanyakan pada dirimu apakah kamu akan menyesal jika berhenti," kata Ratna. "Tanyakan pada dirimu apakah kamu akan menyesal karena tak pernah mencoba."

Armed with a clearer sense of purpose, Farah drafted a resignation letter. She rewrote it four times, agonizing over every sentence, before finally scheduling a meeting with her manager, Pak Hendra, a man who had mentored her for the better part of a decade and whose respect she genuinely valued.

Dibekali dengan tujuan yang lebih jelas, Farah menyusun surat pengunduran diri. Dia menulis ulang empat kali, bersusah payah memikirkan setiap kalimat, sebelum akhirnya menjadwalkan pertemuan dengan manajernya, Pak Hendra, seorang pria yang membimbingnya selama hampir sepuluh tahun dan yang penghormatannya sungguh dia hargai.

When the day arrived, Farah's hands trembled slightly as she knocked on his office door. To her surprise, Pak Hendra listened without interruption, nodding thoughtfully as she explained her reasoning. "I won't pretend I'm not disappointed to lose you," he said finally. "But I'd be a poor mentor if I discouraged you from pursuing something you clearly believe in. Just promise me you'll call if you ever need advice."

Ketika hari itu tiba, tangan Farah sedikit gemetar saat mengetuk pintu ruangannya. Di luar dugaan, Pak Hendra mendengarkan tanpa memotong, mengangguk penuh perenungan saat Farah menjelaskan alasannya. "Aku tidak akan berpura-pura tidak kecewa kehilangan dirimu," katanya akhirnya. "Tapi aku akan jadi mentor yang buruk kalau menghalangimu mengejar sesuatu yang jelas-jelas kamu yakini. Cuma janji padaku, kamu akan menelepon kalau butuh saran."

Farah's last day at the firm was bittersweet, marked by a small farewell gathering and a card signed by nearly every colleague she had worked alongside over the years. Walking out of the building for the final time, she felt an unfamiliar mixture of grief and exhilaration, as though she were simultaneously closing a door and opening a window.

Hari terakhir Farah di perusahaan itu terasa manis sekaligus pahit, ditandai dengan acara perpisahan kecil dan sebuah kartu yang ditandatangani hampir oleh semua rekan kerja yang pernah bekerja bersamanya selama bertahun-tahun. Saat berjalan keluar dari gedung itu untuk yang terakhir kalinya, dia merasakan campuran aneh antara kesedihan dan kegembiraan, seolah dia sedang menutup satu pintu sekaligus membuka satu jendela.

The first few months of running her own consultancy were, as everyone had warned her, grueling. There were nights she questioned whether she had made a catastrophic mistake, and weeks when client payments arrived so late that she had to dip into their savings. Yet there were also small, quietly triumphant moments: her first signed contract, her first client testimonial, the first time she hired an assistant to help manage the growing workload.

Beberapa bulan pertama menjalankan konsultannya sendiri, seperti yang telah diperingatkan semua orang kepadanya, sangatlah berat. Ada malam-malam dia mempertanyakan apakah dia telah membuat kesalahan besar, dan minggu-minggu ketika pembayaran klien datang begitu terlambat sehingga dia harus mengambil dari tabungan mereka. Namun ada pula momen-momen kecil yang diam-diam terasa penuh kemenangan: kontrak pertamanya yang ditandatangani, testimoni klien pertamanya, saat pertama kali dia merekrut asisten untuk membantu mengelola beban kerja yang terus bertambah.

A year later, sitting once again at her kitchen table, Farah reviewed her company's annual figures. The consultancy was not yet as profitable as her old salary had been, but it was growing steadily, and, more importantly, she woke up every morning genuinely eager to begin the day's work. She thought back to Ratna's advice and smiled. Whatever happened next, she knew with absolute certainty that she would never regret having tried.

Setahun kemudian, duduk kembali di meja dapurnya, Farah meninjau angka tahunan perusahaannya. Konsultannya belum seuntung gajinya dulu, tetapi terus tumbuh secara stabil, dan yang lebih penting, dia bangun setiap pagi dengan benar-benar bersemangat untuk memulai pekerjaan hari itu. Dia teringat nasihat Ratna dan tersenyum. Apa pun yang terjadi selanjutnya, dia tahu dengan pasti bahwa dia tidak akan pernah menyesal karena telah mencoba.
