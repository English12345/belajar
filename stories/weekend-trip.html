A Weekend Trip to the Mountains

Liburan Akhir Pekan ke Gunung

💡 Klik kata apa pun untuk melihat artinya, atau tekan "Lihat Arti Cerita" untuk melihat terjemahan tiap paragraf sekaligus.

Rina's family had been planning this trip for weeks. Every day at work felt tiring, so her father suggested a short holiday to the mountains. "We need fresh air," he said. "Let's leave early on Saturday morning."

Keluarga Rina sudah merencanakan perjalanan ini selama berminggu-minggu. Setiap hari kerja terasa melelahkan, jadi ayahnya mengusulkan liburan singkat ke pegunungan. "Kita perlu udara segar," katanya. "Ayo berangkat pagi-pagi Sabtu nanti."

On Saturday, Rina woke up at four in the morning. It was still dark outside. She packed a jacket, a bottle of water, and some snacks into her backpack. Her younger brother, Toni, was already waiting by the car, excited and full of energy.

Sabtu itu, Rina bangun jam empat pagi. Di luar masih gelap. Dia memasukkan jaket, sebotol air, dan beberapa camilan ke dalam ranselnya. Adiknya, Toni, sudah menunggu di dekat mobil, bersemangat dan penuh energi.

The drive to the mountains took about three hours. Along the way, they passed rice fields, small villages, and winding roads. As they got closer, the air became cooler, and thick fog covered the hills. "Look, it's so beautiful!" Toni shouted, pointing out of the window.

Perjalanan ke pegunungan memakan waktu sekitar tiga jam. Sepanjang jalan, mereka melewati sawah, desa-desa kecil, dan jalan berkelok. Semakin dekat, udara semakin sejuk, dan kabut tebal menyelimuti perbukitan. "Lihat, indah sekali!" teriak Toni sambil menunjuk ke luar jendela.

They arrived at a small guesthouse near the foot of the mountain. The owner, a friendly old man, welcomed them with hot tea and fried bananas. "You are lucky," he said. "The weather is clear today. You will have a great view from the top."

Mereka tiba di sebuah penginapan kecil di kaki gunung. Pemiliknya, seorang bapak tua yang ramah, menyambut mereka dengan teh hangat dan pisang goreng. "Kalian beruntung," katanya. "Cuaca hari ini cerah. Kalian akan mendapat pemandangan bagus dari atas."

After breakfast, the family started their hike. The path was steep and rocky in some places, but the view was worth every step. Tall pine trees surrounded the trail, and birds sang in the distance. Rina's mother stopped often to take photos of the wild flowers.

Setelah sarapan, keluarga itu mulai mendaki. Jalannya curam dan berbatu di beberapa bagian, tapi pemandangannya sepadan dengan setiap langkah. Pohon-pohon pinus yang tinggi mengelilingi jalur, dan burung-burung berkicau di kejauhan. Ibu Rina sering berhenti untuk memotret bunga-bunga liar.

"Are you tired?" her father asked Rina as they climbed higher. "A little, but I love it here," she answered, breathing in the fresh, cool air. After almost two hours, they finally reached the top. Below them, clouds floated like a soft white sea, and the sun shone brightly above.

"Capek nggak?" tanya ayahnya kepada Rina saat mereka mendaki lebih tinggi. "Sedikit, tapi aku suka di sini," jawabnya sambil menghirup udara segar dan sejuk. Setelah hampir dua jam, akhirnya mereka sampai di puncak. Di bawah mereka, awan mengambang seperti lautan putih yang lembut, dan matahari bersinar terang di atas.

They sat on a large rock and opened their bags of snacks. "This is the best view I've ever seen," Toni said with his mouth full of bread. Everyone laughed. For a while, nobody talked. They simply enjoyed the silence, the wind, and the wide open sky.

Mereka duduk di atas batu besar dan membuka bekal camilan mereka. "Ini pemandangan terindah yang pernah aku lihat," kata Toni dengan mulut penuh roti. Semua orang tertawa. Sejenak, tak ada yang bicara. Mereka hanya menikmati keheningan, angin, dan langit yang luas terbuka.

In the afternoon, dark clouds appeared, so the family decided to walk back down before the rain started. By the time they reached the guesthouse, they were tired but happy. That night, they sat around a small fire, drank warm chocolate, and shared stories about the day.

Siang harinya, awan gelap mulai muncul, jadi keluarga itu memutuskan untuk turun sebelum hujan datang. Saat mereka sampai kembali di penginapan, mereka lelah namun bahagia. Malam itu, mereka duduk mengelilingi api unggun kecil, minum cokelat hangat, dan bertukar cerita tentang hari itu.

On Sunday morning, before driving home, they walked around the guesthouse garden one last time. "Can we come back next month?" Toni asked. Their father smiled. "Of course. This is definitely not our last trip here."

Minggu pagi, sebelum pulang, mereka berjalan-jalan sekali lagi mengelilingi taman penginapan. "Bisa nggak kita ke sini lagi bulan depan?" tanya Toni. Ayah mereka tersenyum. "Tentu saja. Ini pasti bukan perjalanan terakhir kita ke sini."

As they drove home, Rina looked out of the window and smiled. Sometimes, the best way to feel better is simply to breathe fresh mountain air with the people you love.

Saat mobil melaju pulang, Rina memandang keluar jendela dan tersenyum. Terkadang, cara terbaik untuk merasa lebih baik adalah dengan menghirup udara segar pegunungan bersama orang-orang yang kita sayangi.
