Ordering at a Restaurant

Memesan Makanan di Restoran

💡 Klik kata apa pun untuk melihat artinya, atau tekan "Lihat Arti Cerita" untuk melihat terjemahan tiap paragraf sekaligus.

Andi and Lisa had heard many good things about a new restaurant downtown. On Friday evening, they decided to try it for the first time. The restaurant was cozy, with soft lights and quiet music playing in the background.

Andi dan Lisa sudah banyak mendengar hal-hal bagus tentang restoran baru di pusat kota. Jumat malam, mereka memutuskan untuk mencobanya untuk pertama kali. Restoran itu nyaman, dengan lampu redup dan musik pelan mengalun di latar belakang.

"Good evening, welcome to Sunset Kitchen. Table for two?" the waiter asked with a warm smile. "Yes, please," Andi answered. The waiter led them to a table near the window and handed them two menus. "Take your time. I'll come back in a few minutes to take your order."

"Selamat malam, selamat datang di Sunset Kitchen. Meja untuk dua orang?" tanya pelayan sambil tersenyum hangat. "Iya, tolong," jawab Andi. Pelayan itu mengantar mereka ke meja dekat jendela dan memberikan dua buku menu. "Silakan lihat-lihat dulu. Saya akan kembali beberapa menit lagi untuk mencatat pesanan."

Lisa opened the menu and looked at the pictures carefully. "Everything looks delicious. I can't decide," she said, laughing. "Let's ask for a recommendation," Andi suggested. When the waiter returned, he asked, "Are you ready to order, or do you need a few more minutes?"

Lisa membuka menu dan melihat gambar-gambarnya dengan teliti. "Semuanya kelihatan enak. Aku bingung mau pilih apa," katanya sambil tertawa. "Kita minta rekomendasi saja," usul Andi. Saat pelayan kembali, dia bertanya, "Sudah siap memesan, atau butuh waktu beberapa menit lagi?"

"Actually, could you recommend something?" Lisa asked. "Of course," the waiter said. "Our grilled chicken with black pepper sauce is very popular. We also have a seafood pasta that many customers love." "That sounds great. I'll have the grilled chicken, please," Lisa decided.

"Sebenarnya, boleh minta rekomendasi?" tanya Lisa. "Tentu," kata pelayan itu. "Ayam bakar saus lada hitam kami sangat populer. Kami juga punya pasta seafood yang disukai banyak pelanggan." "Kedengarannya enak. Aku pesan ayam bakarnya ya," putus Lisa.

"And for you, sir?" the waiter asked Andi. "I'll try the seafood pasta. Is it spicy?" Andi asked. "It's mild, but we can make it spicier if you like," the waiter answered. "Yes, please. A little spicy would be perfect," Andi said with a smile.

"Kalau untuk Anda, Pak?" tanya pelayan kepada Andi. "Saya coba pasta seafood-nya. Pedas nggak?" tanya Andi. "Rasanya ringan, tapi bisa kami buat lebih pedas kalau Anda mau," jawab pelayan. "Iya tolong, sedikit pedas pas sekali," kata Andi sambil tersenyum.

"Would you like anything to drink?" the waiter continued. "Can I have an iced lemon tea?" Lisa asked. "And I'll have a glass of orange juice, please," Andi added. The waiter wrote down their order and said, "Great choices. Your food will be ready in about fifteen minutes."

"Mau minum apa?" lanjut pelayan itu. "Boleh minta es teh lemon?" tanya Lisa. "Saya jus jeruk saja," tambah Andi. Pelayan mencatat pesanan mereka dan berkata, "Pilihan yang bagus. Makanannya akan siap sekitar lima belas menit lagi."

While waiting, Andi and Lisa talked about their busy week at work. Soon, the waiter came back with their drinks. "Here you go. Your food is almost ready," he said, placing the glasses on the table.

Sambil menunggu, Andi dan Lisa mengobrol tentang minggu kerja mereka yang sibuk. Tak lama, pelayan kembali membawa minuman mereka. "Ini minumannya. Makanannya hampir siap," katanya sambil meletakkan gelas di meja.

A few minutes later, two plates of hot food arrived. "This smells amazing," Lisa said, picking up her fork. They both took a bite. "Wow, this chicken is really good," Lisa said happily. "The pasta is perfect too, not too spicy, not too mild," Andi agreed.

Beberapa menit kemudian, dua piring makanan hangat pun tiba. "Baunya enak sekali," kata Lisa sambil mengambil garpunya. Mereka berdua mencicipi. "Wah, ayamnya enak banget," kata Lisa dengan senang. "Pastanya juga pas, nggak terlalu pedas, nggak terlalu hambar," Andi setuju.

After they finished eating, the waiter came to clear the table. "How was everything?" he asked. "It was absolutely delicious. We'll definitely come back," Andi said. "Could we have the bill, please?" Lisa asked politely.

Setelah selesai makan, pelayan datang membereskan meja. "Bagaimana makanannya?" tanyanya. "Enak sekali. Kami pasti akan kembali lagi," kata Andi. "Boleh minta bonnya?" tanya Lisa dengan sopan.

The waiter brought the bill a moment later. Andi paid at the counter and left a small tip for good service. "Thank you for coming tonight. Have a wonderful evening," the waiter said as they walked out. "Thank you, we really enjoyed it," Lisa replied with a smile.

Pelayan membawakan bon tak lama kemudian. Andi membayar di kasir dan meninggalkan tip kecil untuk pelayanan yang baik. "Terima kasih sudah datang malam ini. Selamat malam," kata pelayan saat mereka berjalan keluar. "Terima kasih, kami sangat menikmatinya," jawab Lisa sambil tersenyum.

Walking home under the streetlights, Andi and Lisa agreed that Sunset Kitchen would now be one of their favorite places to eat.

Berjalan pulang di bawah lampu jalan, Andi dan Lisa sepakat bahwa Sunset Kitchen kini menjadi salah satu tempat makan favorit mereka.
