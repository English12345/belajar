The New Neighbor

Tetangga Baru

💡 Klik kata apa pun untuk melihat artinya, atau tekan "Lihat Arti Cerita" untuk melihat terjemahan tiap paragraf sekaligus.

Mira lived in a small house at the end of Melati Street. On Saturday morning, she heard a truck outside. She looked out of the window and saw boxes, a sofa, and a bicycle. "Someone is moving in next door," she thought.

Mira tinggal di rumah kecil di ujung Jalan Melati. Sabtu pagi, dia mendengar suara truk di luar. Dia melihat ke luar jendela dan melihat kardus-kardus, sofa, dan sepeda. "Sepertinya ada yang pindah rumah di sebelah," pikirnya.

Later that day, Mira decided to say hello. She walked to the house next door and knocked on the door. A young woman opened it. She had a friendly smile and short black hair.

Hari itu juga, Mira memutuskan untuk menyapa. Dia berjalan ke rumah sebelah dan mengetuk pintu. Seorang wanita muda membukanya. Dia memiliki senyum yang ramah dan rambut hitam pendek.

"Hi there," the woman said. "Sorry, the house is still a mess. We just moved in this morning."

"Hai," kata wanita itu. "Maaf, rumahnya masih berantakan. Kami baru saja pindah tadi pagi."

"No problem at all," Mira replied. "My name is Mira. I live right next door. Welcome to the neighborhood!"

"Tidak apa-apa," jawab Mira. "Namaku Mira. Aku tinggal persis di sebelah. Selamat datang di lingkungan ini!"

"Thank you so much. My name is Dewi. It's nice to meet you," said the woman. She shook Mira's hand.

"Terima kasih banyak. Namaku Dewi. Senang berkenalan denganmu," kata wanita itu. Dia berjabat tangan dengan Mira.

"Where are you from?" Mira asked. "I'm from Surabaya," Dewi said. "I moved here because of my new job. I work at a hospital downtown."

"Kamu berasal dari mana?" tanya Mira. "Aku dari Surabaya," kata Dewi. "Aku pindah ke sini karena pekerjaan baruku. Aku bekerja di rumah sakit di pusat kota."

"That's great. Are you a doctor?" Mira asked with curiosity. "Not exactly," Dewi laughed. "I'm a nurse. I start next Monday, so I still have a few days to unpack."

"Wah, bagus sekali. Apakah kamu seorang dokter?" tanya Mira penasaran. "Bukan," Dewi tertawa. "Aku seorang perawat. Aku mulai kerja Senin depan, jadi masih ada beberapa hari untuk beres-beres."

Mira looked at the boxes in the living room. "Do you need any help? I'm free this afternoon," she offered. "Really? That would be wonderful," Dewi said. "I have so many boxes and I don't know where to start."

Mira melihat kardus-kardus di ruang tamu. "Butuh bantuan? Aku senggang sore ini," tawarnya. "Sungguh? Itu akan sangat membantu," kata Dewi. "Kardusku banyak sekali dan aku bingung mau mulai dari mana."

They spent the afternoon together. Mira helped Dewi carry boxes into the kitchen and the bedroom. They talked about their families, their favorite food, and their daily routines. Mira learned that Dewi liked to cook, and Dewi learned that Mira worked as a teacher at a nearby school.

Mereka menghabiskan sore itu bersama. Mira membantu Dewi membawa kardus ke dapur dan kamar tidur. Mereka mengobrol tentang keluarga, makanan favorit, dan kegiatan sehari-hari. Mira tahu bahwa Dewi suka memasak, dan Dewi tahu bahwa Mira bekerja sebagai guru di sekolah dekat situ.

By evening, the living room looked much better. "Thank you for your help today," Dewi said. "Would you like to stay for dinner? It's nothing fancy, just fried rice." "I would love that," Mira answered with a smile.

Menjelang malam, ruang tamu terlihat jauh lebih rapi. "Terima kasih untuk bantuanmu hari ini," kata Dewi. "Mau makan malam di sini? Sederhana saja, nasi goreng." "Aku mau sekali," jawab Mira sambil tersenyum.

They sat together in the small kitchen and ate dinner. Outside, the sky turned orange, then purple, then dark blue. It was the beginning of a new friendship. From that day, Mira and Dewi became good neighbors. They often had coffee together in the morning and helped each other whenever they could.

Mereka duduk bersama di dapur kecil dan makan malam. Di luar, langit berubah oranye, lalu ungu, lalu biru gelap. Itulah awal dari sebuah persahabatan baru. Sejak hari itu, Mira dan Dewi menjadi tetangga yang akrab. Mereka sering minum kopi bersama di pagi hari dan saling membantu kapan pun bisa.

Sometimes a simple "hello" is the first step to a new friend.

Terkadang, sapaan sederhana "halo" adalah langkah pertama menuju teman baru.
