A Day at the Market

Sehari di Pasar

💡 Klik kata apa pun untuk melihat artinya, atau tekan "Lihat Arti Cerita" untuk melihat terjemahan tiap paragraf sekaligus.

Every Sunday morning, Sari wakes up early to go to the traditional market. She likes to arrive before seven o'clock, when the vegetables and fish are still fresh. This morning, she takes her bag and a small list of things to buy.

Setiap Minggu pagi, Sari bangun pagi-pagi untuk pergi ke pasar tradisional. Dia suka tiba sebelum jam tujuh, saat sayur dan ikan masih segar. Pagi ini, dia membawa tasnya dan daftar kecil belanjaan.

The market is busy and noisy. Sellers shout the prices of their goods. The smell of fresh bread mixes with the smell of spices and fried snacks. Sari walks slowly between the stalls, looking at the colorful fruits and vegetables.

Pasar itu ramai dan berisik. Para pedagang berteriak menawarkan harga barang mereka. Aroma roti segar bercampur dengan aroma rempah dan gorengan. Sari berjalan pelan di antara lapak-lapak, melihat buah dan sayur yang berwarna-warni.

First, she stops at a vegetable stall. "Good morning, how much are the tomatoes?" she asks. "Fifteen thousand rupiah for one kilogram," the seller answers. "That's a bit expensive. Can you make it twelve thousand?" Sari asks politely. The seller smiles. "Okay, just for you, twelve thousand."

Pertama, dia berhenti di lapak sayur. "Selamat pagi, tomatnya berapa?" tanyanya. "Lima belas ribu rupiah per kilo," jawab penjualnya. "Agak mahal ya. Bisa dua belas ribu?" tanya Sari dengan sopan. Penjual itu tersenyum. "Baik, khusus untukmu, dua belas ribu."

Sari buys one kilogram of tomatoes, a bunch of spinach, and some carrots. She puts them carefully into her bag. Next, she walks to the fish stall. The fish are lying on ice, still shining and fresh.

Sari membeli satu kilo tomat, seikat bayam, dan beberapa wortel. Dia memasukkannya dengan hati-hati ke dalam tas. Selanjutnya, dia berjalan ke lapak ikan. Ikan-ikan itu tergeletak di atas es, masih mengkilap dan segar.

"Do you have any fresh fish today?" she asks the fish seller. "Yes, we have milkfish and mackerel. Both arrived this morning," he replies. "I'll take one mackerel, please. Not too big." The seller weighs the fish and wraps it in plastic.

"Ada ikan segar hari ini?" tanyanya kepada penjual ikan. "Ada, kami punya bandeng dan kembung. Keduanya baru datang tadi pagi," jawabnya. "Saya ambil satu kembung ya, jangan yang terlalu besar." Penjual itu menimbang ikannya dan membungkusnya dengan plastik.

After that, Sari visits the fruit stall. She buys some bananas, a papaya, and a few mangoes. The fruit seller gives her a free banana. "This is a gift for you," he says with a warm smile. "Thank you very much!" Sari says happily.

Setelah itu, Sari mampir ke lapak buah. Dia membeli beberapa pisang, satu pepaya, dan beberapa mangga. Penjual buah itu memberinya satu pisang gratis. "Ini hadiah untukmu," katanya sambil tersenyum hangat. "Terima kasih banyak!" kata Sari dengan gembira.

Near the end of the market, there is a small stall selling hot drinks and snacks. Sari feels a little tired, so she stops to rest. She orders a glass of hot tea and a piece of fried banana. She sits on a wooden bench and watches the people walking by.

Di ujung pasar, ada lapak kecil yang menjual minuman hangat dan camilan. Sari merasa sedikit lelah, jadi dia berhenti untuk istirahat. Dia memesan segelas teh hangat dan sepotong pisang goreng. Dia duduk di bangku kayu dan memperhatikan orang-orang yang lalu lalang.

An old woman sits next to her. "Are you buying a lot today?" the woman asks kindly. "Yes, I'm cooking for my family tonight," Sari answers. "That sounds nice. What are you cooking?" the woman asks. "Fish soup and stir-fried vegetables," Sari says.

Seorang wanita tua duduk di sebelahnya. "Belanja banyak hari ini?" tanya wanita itu dengan ramah. "Iya, saya mau masak untuk keluarga nanti malam," jawab Sari. "Wah, enak. Mau masak apa?" tanya wanita itu. "Sup ikan dan tumis sayur," kata Sari.

They talk for a few minutes about recipes and family. Then Sari finishes her tea, pays for her snack, and picks up her bags. It is almost nine o'clock, and the sun is getting hot. She walks home slowly, thinking about what to cook for dinner.

Mereka mengobrol beberapa menit tentang resep dan keluarga. Kemudian Sari menghabiskan tehnya, membayar jajanannya, dan mengangkat tas belanjaannya. Hampir jam sembilan, dan matahari mulai terasa panas. Dia berjalan pulang perlahan, memikirkan apa yang akan dimasak untuk makan malam.

Going to the market is more than just shopping. For Sari, it is also a chance to meet people and enjoy a simple, happy morning.

Pergi ke pasar bukan sekadar berbelanja. Bagi Sari, ini juga kesempatan untuk bertemu orang-orang dan menikmati pagi yang sederhana namun menyenangkan.
