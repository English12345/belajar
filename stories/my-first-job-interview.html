My First Job Interview

Wawancara Kerja Pertamaku

💡 Klik kata apa pun untuk melihat artinya, atau tekan "Lihat Arti Cerita" untuk melihat terjemahan tiap paragraf sekaligus.

Dimas had never been so nervous in his life. Today was his first job interview after graduating from university. He arrived at the office thirty minutes early, wearing a clean white shirt and black trousers. His hands were slightly shaking as he sat in the waiting room.

Dimas belum pernah segugup ini seumur hidupnya. Hari ini adalah wawancara kerja pertamanya setelah lulus kuliah. Dia tiba di kantor tiga puluh menit lebih awal, mengenakan kemeja putih bersih dan celana hitam. Tangannya sedikit gemetar saat dia duduk di ruang tunggu.

A receptionist called his name. "Mr. Dimas? The manager is ready to see you now. Please follow me." Dimas stood up, took a deep breath, and walked into a bright meeting room. A woman in a navy blue blazer greeted him with a smile.

Seorang resepsionis memanggil namanya. "Pak Dimas? Manajernya sudah siap menemui Anda sekarang. Silakan ikuti saya." Dimas berdiri, menarik napas dalam-dalam, dan berjalan masuk ke ruang rapat yang terang. Seorang wanita berblazer biru dongker menyambutnya dengan senyuman.

"Good morning, Dimas. Thank you for coming. Please, have a seat," she said. "I'm Ms. Ayu, the human resources manager." "Good morning, Ms. Ayu. Thank you for the opportunity," Dimas replied, trying to sound confident.

"Selamat pagi, Dimas. Terima kasih sudah datang. Silakan duduk," katanya. "Saya Bu Ayu, manajer sumber daya manusia." "Selamat pagi, Bu Ayu. Terima kasih atas kesempatannya," jawab Dimas, berusaha terdengar percaya diri.

Ms. Ayu opened his resume on her laptop. "So, tell me a little about yourself," she began. Dimas had practiced this question many times. "My name is Dimas. I recently graduated with a degree in marketing. During my studies, I did an internship at a small advertising company, where I learned how to manage social media accounts."

Bu Ayu membuka CV-nya di laptop. "Coba ceritakan sedikit tentang diri Anda," dia memulai. Dimas sudah berlatih menjawab pertanyaan ini berkali-kali. "Nama saya Dimas. Saya baru lulus dengan gelar di bidang pemasaran. Selama kuliah, saya magang di perusahaan periklanan kecil, di sana saya belajar mengelola akun media sosial."

"That's interesting," Ms. Ayu said, taking a note. "Why do you want to work for our company?" she asked next. "I've followed your company for a long time," Dimas answered. "I admire how creative your advertising campaigns are, and I would love to be part of a team like that."

"Menarik sekali," kata Bu Ayu sambil mencatat. "Mengapa Anda ingin bekerja di perusahaan kami?" tanyanya selanjutnya. "Saya sudah mengikuti perusahaan ini sejak lama," jawab Dimas. "Saya kagum dengan kreativitas kampanye iklan kalian, dan saya ingin sekali menjadi bagian dari tim seperti itu."

"What are your strengths?" Ms. Ayu continued. "I think I'm a fast learner, and I work well under pressure. In my internship, I often had to finish tasks with very short deadlines, and I always tried my best to deliver good results," Dimas explained.

"Apa kelebihan Anda?" lanjut Bu Ayu. "Saya rasa saya cepat belajar, dan saya bisa bekerja baik di bawah tekanan. Saat magang, saya sering harus menyelesaikan tugas dengan tenggat waktu yang sangat singkat, dan saya selalu berusaha memberikan hasil terbaik," jelas Dimas.

"And what about your weaknesses?" she asked. Dimas paused for a moment. "Sometimes I focus too much on small details, so I'm learning to manage my time better and see the bigger picture," he said honestly.

"Kalau kekurangan Anda?" tanyanya. Dimas terdiam sejenak. "Terkadang saya terlalu fokus pada hal-hal kecil, jadi saya sedang belajar mengatur waktu dengan lebih baik dan melihat gambaran besarnya," katanya jujur.

Ms. Ayu smiled and nodded. "That's a very honest answer. Do you have any questions for me?" she asked at the end. "Yes," Dimas said. "What would a normal day look like in this position?" Ms. Ayu explained the daily tasks, the team he would work with, and the training program for new employees.

Bu Ayu tersenyum dan mengangguk. "Jawaban yang sangat jujur. Apakah Anda punya pertanyaan untuk saya?" tanyanya di akhir. "Ya," kata Dimas. "Seperti apa hari kerja biasa untuk posisi ini?" Bu Ayu menjelaskan tugas hariannya, tim yang akan dia ajak bekerja, dan program pelatihan untuk karyawan baru.

After twenty minutes, the interview ended. "Thank you for your time, Dimas. We will contact you next week with our decision," Ms. Ayu said, shaking his hand. "Thank you very much for this opportunity. I hope to hear good news soon," Dimas replied politely.

Setelah dua puluh menit, wawancara pun berakhir. "Terima kasih atas waktunya, Dimas. Kami akan menghubungi Anda minggu depan dengan keputusan kami," kata Bu Ayu sambil berjabat tangan. "Terima kasih banyak atas kesempatan ini. Saya harap segera mendengar kabar baik," jawab Dimas dengan sopan.

Walking out of the building, Dimas felt relieved. He did not know if he would get the job, but he knew he had done his best. A week later, he received an email: "We are happy to offer you the position." Dimas smiled from ear to ear. His hard work had finally paid off.

Berjalan keluar dari gedung, Dimas merasa lega. Dia tidak tahu apakah akan mendapatkan pekerjaan itu, tapi dia tahu dia sudah berusaha sebaik mungkin. Seminggu kemudian, dia menerima email: "Kami dengan senang hati menawarkan posisi ini kepada Anda." Dimas tersenyum lebar. Kerja kerasnya akhirnya membuahkan hasil.
